Our Story

Established in 1996 in the city of Dammam, Badr Medical Group has steadily earned a distinguished reputation for excellence in healthcare delivery. Our commitment to quality, integrity, and continuous improvement has guided our expansion into Qatif (2009), Jubail (2017), and recent addition in Khobar (2025).

We take pride in our team of experienced healthcare professionals, many of whom bring over 25 years of expertise in their respective fields. Supported by a multicultural and multilingual workforce, we ensure that our care is not only medically sound but also culturally attuned and patient-focused.

As we broaden our scope throughout the Kingdom, our initiatives remain aligned with Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030, reflecting our dedication to advancing national healthcare standards and serving communities with excellence and compassion.

Our Vision

To provide excellent health care services delivered by qualified and experienced medical team, becoming progressive and compassionate in service, ensuring every patient achieves their best state of care possible.

Our Mission

Delivering premium healthcare with efficiency by integrating innovation, expertise and compassion, ensuring every patient experiences their best state of care possible.

Our Values

To uphold Dignity, Compassion, Teamwork, Excellence, Innovation and Quality in every aspect of our care and services.

Commitment to Quality Healthcare

Badr Medical Group is dedicated to delivering high-quality healthcare at an affordable cost, ensuring accessibility for all. We strive to deliver the highest level of comprehensive care to our patients, and we continue to expand our services and coverage to cater to the growing medical needs of our community.

We take pride in being at the forefront of medical advances, continuously pushing boundaries to create relevant and inventive solutions in healthcare sector.
